# CourierLedger — Day 7 outreach messages (copy-paste)

Use these by hand, one seller at a time. No automation, no bulk sending. At most two contacts per seller, then stop. No product description in any message. If a seller asks what the research is for, answer: "To understand whether checking courier payments is a real problem for sellers, before deciding whether anything should be built for it." Do not name features, prices or timelines. No incentive is promised in any message — see pack §1.5; if Naim decides on one, add the same wording to every message before the first one goes out.

Replace `[Your name]` with Naim's name. Keep the 15–20 minute length in every message.

---

## 1. Initial message — English

> Hello, I am researching how online sellers in Bangladesh check the payments they receive from courier companies for cash-on-delivery orders. I am speaking with sellers for 15–20 minutes about how they did this most recently. I am not selling anything, and you would not need to share any account details or statements. Would you be open to a short conversation this week? I can work around your schedule. — [Your name]

## 2. Initial message — Bangla (Roman)

> Assalamu alaikum, ami Bangladesh-er online seller-ra courier company theke cash-on-delivery order-er payment kivabe check koren, sheta niye research korchi. Apni shobcheye recently kivabe check korechilen, sheta niye 15–20 minute kotha bolte chai. Ami kichu bechchi na, ar apnar account-er details ba statement share korte hobe na. Ei week-e ektu shomoy dite parben? Apnar shubidha moto shomoy-e kotha bolbo. — [Your name]

## 3. Initial message — Bangla (script)

> আসসালামু আলাইকুম, আমি বাংলাদেশের অনলাইন সেলাররা কুরিয়ার কোম্পানি থেকে ক্যাশ-অন-ডেলিভারি অর্ডারের পেমেন্ট কীভাবে যাচাই করেন, সেটা নিয়ে গবেষণা করছি। আপনি সবচেয়ে সম্প্রতি কীভাবে যাচাই করেছিলেন, সেটা নিয়ে ১৫–২০ মিনিট কথা বলতে চাই। আমি কিছু বিক্রি করছি না, আর আপনার অ্যাকাউন্টের তথ্য বা স্টেটমেন্ট শেয়ার করতে হবে না। এই সপ্তাহে একটু সময় দিতে পারবেন? আপনার সুবিধামতো সময়ে কথা বলব। — [Your name]

## 6. If they ask "what is this for?" — English / Bangla (Roman)

## 7. If they ask "do I have to share my statements?" — English / Bangla (Roman)

> No — please do not share account numbers, customer details or full statements. I only want to hear, in your own words, how you checked your most recent courier payment.
>
> Na — account number, customer-er details ba puro statement share korben na. Ami shudhu apnar mukhe shunte chai, shobcheye recent courier payment-ta apni kivabe check korechilen.

If you sell online in Bangladesh and use a courier for COD, I would like to ask you 8 short screening questions and, if you qualify, speak for 15–20 minutes about how you checked your most recent courier payment. No account details or statements needed. Please comment or DM me if you are open to it. Thank you!
